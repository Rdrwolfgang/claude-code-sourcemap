How to Play

Design with an Apple Pencil Pro

Pencil only

The canvas only accepts marks from an actual pencil — fingers and mice are refused so nothing gets a "hand mark." Field patterns, layers, colors and the tray are normal touch UI.

Pressure

Push harder for a bolder, more opaque stroke — lighter for a thin, faint line.

Tilt

Lay the pencil over and your stroke gains a shaded, graphite-like edge — like shading with the side of a lead.

Barrel roll (azimuth)

Hover the Stamp tool and roll the pencil in your fingers — the symbol preview spins live to match. Lift to commit it at that angle.

Squeeze

Real Pencil Pro squeeze isn't opened up to web pages yet, so it's emulated: press suddenly and sharply mid-stroke to pop the radial tool picker at your tip. The Squeeze Pad button always works too, as a guaranteed fallback.
